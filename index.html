About Our Company

KRUPA Fasteners is a trusted stockist and wholesaler of stainless steel fasteners, brass turned components, and high-performance industrial fastening solutions serving the pump manufacturing and allied engineering industries in Coimbatore.

With over 30 years of industrial experience, we successfully meet diverse customer requirements by offering extensive sizes availability, vast ready stocks, highly competitive pricing, and prompt delivery schedules.

We also specialize in high-precision customization as per customer drawings, designing and supplying special components to meet unique industrial engineering specifications. Our core commitment is to deliver outstanding product quality with reliable, direct service to build trusted, long-term customer relationships.
