About Our Company

Welcome to KRUPA Fasteners, your premier destination for high-performance industrial fastening solutions. Based in Coimbatore, we specialize in the manufacturing and distribution of a comprehensive range of precision-engineered nuts, bolts, screws, and specialized fasteners.

With a steadfast commitment to quality, all our products adhere to stringent DIN and ISO standards. We are specialists in SS and Brass Turned Components as per drawing, ensuring maximum reliability for the most demanding applications in automotive, construction, and heavy engineering sectors. Our mission is to empower global industries by providing durable, high-quality hardware.
